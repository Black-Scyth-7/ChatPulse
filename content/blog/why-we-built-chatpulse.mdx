Every team we've worked on has had the same quiet complaint: chat is where
work gets discussed, and also where work gets lost. The channel that was
supposed to keep everyone in sync becomes a wall of noise. The important
decision from Tuesday is buried under three days of GIFs, standup threads, and
a deploy that went sideways at 2am.

We didn't set out to build another messaging app. We set out to fix the
feeling that you're always slightly behind.

## The problem isn't chat. It's the noise around it.

Modern team chat optimizes for engagement. More notifications, more channels,
more reasons to check back in. That's great for a product's daily active
users. It's terrible for a team trying to ship.

We kept seeing the same three failures:

- **Signal buried in noise.** The message that mattered scrolled past while
  you were in a meeting.
- **Context that doesn't travel.** A decision lives in one thread, in one
  channel, invisible to the person who needs it next week.
- **Privacy as an afterthought.** Sensitive conversations sitting in plaintext
  on someone else's server, indexed and retained indefinitely.

## What we're building instead

ChatPulse starts from a different premise: your team's attention is the
scarcest resource you have, and your conversations belong to you.

That shows up in three commitments:

1. **Calm by default.** Notifications are earned, not assumed. We surface what
   changed since you were last here, not everything that happened.
2. **Private by design.** Team conversations are end-to-end encrypted, so the
   content of your messages is readable by your team and no one else — not
   even us.
3. **Built for the people who build.** A real API, real webhooks, and a CLI,
   because the teams we care about most live in their tools.

## Where we're headed

This is the first post on the ChatPulse blog, and it's a promise as much as an
introduction. We're going to build in the open, write about the hard parts,
and keep the product honest about the one thing that matters: helping your
team hear each other clearly.
