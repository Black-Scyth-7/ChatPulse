## 2. Positioning statement (internal, XYZ form)

For **dev teams, startups, and remote-first companies** who are frustrated that mainstream chat tools bury decisions in noise and treat security as an add-on, **ChatPulse** is a **real-time team messaging platform** that delivers **fast, searchable, end-to-end-encrypted conversation** so teams can move quickly without leaking context — or trust. Unlike heavyweight enterprise suites and consumer-grade chat apps, ChatPulse pairs a clean, developer-native experience with encryption and search as first-class features, not paid tiers.

---

## 3. Value propositions

Three pillars. Every benefit and feature ladders up to one of these.

1. **Fast conversations that don't lose context.** Real-time messaging plus threads and channels keep discussion structured, so decisions stay findable instead of scrolling away.
2. **Private by default.** End-to-end encryption is on from message one — not a compliance upsell. Your conversations, files, and search index stay yours.
3. **Built to fit an engineering workflow.** Full-text search, file sharing, and integrations meet dev teams where they already work — clean, keyboard-fast, and low-noise.

## 5. Target audience

Primary segments, in priority order. Copy should feel written _for_ these readers.

### Dev teams (primary)

- **Who:** Engineers, EMs, and platform/infra folks who live in chat during builds, reviews, and incidents.
- **Care about:** Speed, keyboard-first UX, searchable history, low noise, and not being sold enterprise bloat.
- **Message emphasis:** Real-time + search + threads. "Chat that respects how engineers work."

### Startups (primary)

- **Who:** Small, fast-moving teams (2–50) standing up their tooling.
- **Care about:** Getting running in minutes, security they don't have to configure, tools that scale with them.
- **Message emphasis:** E2E encryption by default + fast setup. "Enterprise-grade privacy without the enterprise setup."

### Remote-first companies (primary)

- **Who:** Distributed teams where chat _is_ the office.
- **Care about:** Async-friendly structure (threads/channels), reliable history, and trust across borders and time zones.
- **Message emphasis:** Threads/channels + search + encryption. "When chat is your office, it should be private and findable."

**Anti-audience (who we're not for, to keep positioning sharp):** large enterprises seeking a compliance-heavy suite with admin sprawl; consumer social users. We stay lean and developer-native.

## 6. Voice & tone guide

**Voice = clean, confident, slightly technical.** Think Linear / Vercel, not enterprise SaaS.

### Principles

1. **Clear over clever.** Lead with the benefit; say what it does in plain words. Cut throat-clearing.
2. **Confident, not loud.** State value as fact. No hype words, no exclamation-mark energy, no fake urgency.
3. **Technically fluent, never jargon-drunk.** We can say "end-to-end encryption," "full-text search," "threads." We don't hide behind buzzwords ("synergy," "revolutionary," "next-gen").
4. **Respect the reader's time.** Short sentences. Active voice. One idea per line.
5. **Honest.** Never oversell unshipped features or invent metrics.

### Mechanics

- **Person:** Second person ("you," "your team"). Product is "ChatPulse," not "we" in body copy.
- **Tense:** Present. "ChatPulse encrypts every message," not "will encrypt."
- **Sentence length:** Mostly short. Vary for rhythm. Headlines can be fragments.
- **Capitalization:** Sentence case for headings and buttons. Product name is "ChatPulse" (one word, camel-cap P).
- **Punctuation:** Serial comma. Em dashes for asides, sparingly. Avoid exclamation marks.
- **Numbers:** Numerals for stats ("sub-second," "3 clicks"). Spell out one–nine in prose when not a metric.

### Word list

- **Use:** fast, real-time, secure, encrypted, private, searchable, clean, threads, channels, ship, focus, signal.
- **Avoid:** revolutionary, cutting-edge, seamless (overused), leverage, synergy, game-changing, world-class, best-in-class, disrupt, unleash, supercharge.

### Do / Don't

| Don't                                                 | Do                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| "Revolutionize the way your team communicates!"       | "Team chat that keeps up with your team."    |
| "We leverage cutting-edge E2E encryption technology." | "Every message is encrypted end to end."     |
| "Seamlessly search across all your data silos."       | "Search every message and file — instantly." |

Pick one headline + subhead pairing at design/build time; all are on-brand.

- **A (benefit-led, recommended):** "Team chat that's fast, searchable, and private."
- **B (audience-led):** "Real-time chat built for engineering teams."
- **C (security-led):** "Every message, encrypted end to end."
- **D (outcome-led):** "Keep the signal. Lose the noise."

### Subhead options (pair with the headline)

- **1 (recommended, pairs with A/B):** "ChatPulse gives dev teams real-time messaging, threads, and full-text search — with end-to-end encryption on by default. Fast to set up, built to stay out of your way."
- **2 (security emphasis, pairs with C):** "Real-time messaging, threads, channels, and instant search — all end-to-end encrypted, so your team moves fast without giving up privacy."
- **3 (concise, pairs with any):** "Fast, searchable, end-to-end-encrypted team chat for dev teams, startups, and remote-first companies."

### Recommended default hero

> **Headline:** Team chat that's fast, searchable, and private.
> **Subhead:** ChatPulse gives dev teams real-time messaging, threads, and full-text search — with end-to-end encryption on by default. Fast to set up, built to stay out of your way.

### Primary CTA

- **Primary (recommended):** **"Join the waitlist"**
- Supporting microcopy under the CTA: "Be first in when we launch. No spam, unsubscribe anytime."
- **Secondary CTA (optional):** "See how it works" (anchor to features) or "Read the blog."

**CTA button conventions:** sentence case, verb-first, ≤3 words. Approved set: "Join the waitlist," "Get early access," "Read the blog," "See how it works." Avoid "Sign up free" (no pricing claim yet) and "Get started" (no product to start yet).
