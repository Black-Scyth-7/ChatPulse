## Product name

**ChatPulse**

## Tagline

Real-time analytics for every conversation you ship.

## Product description

ChatPulse is a real-time analytics layer for chat and messaging products. Drop in one SDK and get live dashboards for message volume, response latency, active conversations, and drop-off — no data pipeline to build, no warehouse to wire up. Teams see what's happening inside their chat the moment it happens, catch broken flows before users churn, and turn raw conversation traffic into decisions instead of guesswork.

## Target audience

Product and engineering teams that ship chat as a core surface: support platforms, community and social apps, in-product messaging, and AI chat interfaces. The buyer is a developer or product owner at a startup, scale-up, or remote-first company who already instruments their app and wants conversation-level visibility without standing up an analytics stack to get it.

## Value propositions

- **Live, not lagging.** Metrics stream in real time, so you see spikes, stalls, and outages as they happen — not in tomorrow's report.
- **Instrument in an afternoon.** One SDK, a few lines of code. No ETL, no schema design, no dedicated data engineer.
- **Conversation-native metrics.** Latency, resolution, hand-off, and drop-off measured the way chat actually works — not generic pageview events forced onto a messaging flow.
- **Built to grow with you.** From your first thousand messages to millions a day, the same setup holds — no re-platforming as volume climbs.

## Competitive differentiators

- **Chat-first, not retrofitted.** General product analytics tools treat a conversation as a bag of clicks. ChatPulse models threads, turns, and response times as first-class data.
- **Real-time by default.** Most analytics tools batch. ChatPulse is built on a streaming core, so "real-time" is the baseline behavior, not a premium add-on.
- **Developer-owned setup.** No sales-gated onboarding or implementation services. Read the docs, drop in the SDK, ship — the way developers expect tooling to work.
- **Signal over dashboards.** We surface the handful of conversation metrics that predict retention and reliability, instead of burying them under a hundred vanity charts.
